Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how Kimaru & Kiplagat Advocates collects, uses and protects personal information.

1. Who we are

Kimaru & Kiplagat Advocates is a law firm based in Eldoret, Kenya. This website is operated by the firm. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean the firm.

2. Information we collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to give us. This can include:

  • Your name and phone number when you call or message us, including through WhatsApp.
  • Your email address when you email us.
  • Any details you choose to share about your legal matter.

This website does not currently have sign-up or contact forms.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation.
  • Provide legal services and communicate with you about your matter.
  • Meet our legal and professional obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Confidentiality and sharing

Advocates are bound by a duty of confidentiality, and we treat what you tell us as confidential. We share personal information only where you ask us to, where the law requires it, or where it is needed to handle your matter, for example with a court or another professional acting for you.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, your messages are also handled by WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy. Please avoid sending sensitive documents until an advocate has spoken with you.

5. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have rights over your personal information. You may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your information, where the law allows.
  • Stop using your information in a particular way.

To make a request, contact us using the details in the next section.
